Restaurant QR FAQ Demo Page

This is a sample page where guests can quickly check common questions by scanning a table QR code.

How to Use

  1. Place QR codes on tables, at the entrance, and on menus
  2. Guests scan the code using their smartphones
  3. Guests solve simple questions through the FAQ and ask staff only when needed

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Please tell our staff the menu item name. If mobile ordering is available, follow the in-store instructions.

Can you accommodate food allergies?

We will do our best. Please inform us of any allergens (such as eggs, dairy, wheat, or nuts) before ordering.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit cards, and transit IC cards. Please check the register display for QR payment availability.

Can I get an English menu?

Yes. Please ask our staff for an English menu.

How do I ask for the bill?

Please tell the staff, "Could I have the bill, please?"
